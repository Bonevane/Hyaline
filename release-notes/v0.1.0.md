The first public build of Hyaline: live desktop widgets for your Mac, made of real Liquid Glass, that sit beside Apple's own.

## What's in it

- **63 widgets in 16 groups:** clocks (including a lock-screen clock with numerals made of glass), mood, CPU and system, memory, battery, network, storage, energy, temperatures, time, calendar, reminders, weather, Now Playing, notes, script output, GitHub and feeds.
- **Real Liquid Glass**, shaded to match Apple's widgets side by side. Each widget can also be Frosted or Clear.
- **At home next to Apple's widgets:** the same sizes, snapping to their grid and gutters and to the screen's centre, no overlaps. Two slim widgets stack to a medium, and a medium plus two slims to a large.
- **Behaves like Apple's widgets** when you swipe between desktops and full-screen apps, in Mission Control, and when you click the wallpaper.
- **Layouts that survive resolution changes**, following Apple's widgets as macOS moves them.
- **Widget Gallery:** every widget live at its real size. Click to add, drag onto the desktop to place, or pick its style and options first.
- **Click to open:** each widget opens its app or the matching System Settings pane.
- **Easy on the battery:** everything still updates on battery, just less often.
- **Open at Login** and **updates from GitHub**, checked daily and installed when you say so.

## Install

1. Download `Hyaline-0.1.0.dmg`, open it and drag Hyaline onto Applications.
2. Open Hyaline. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS refuses. Click **Done**, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Hyaline.
3. Click the Hyaline icon in the menu bar and choose **Widget Gallery…** to add widgets. macOS asks for Calendar, Reminders, Location or Automation the first time a widget needs it.

Later versions install themselves from **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
